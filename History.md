# History

## HPC in Canada

Dr. Mark Whitmore, one of ACENET\'s founders has co-authored a paper with Allan MacIsaac on the history of High Performance Computing in
Canada. [High Performance Computing in Canada: The Early Chapters](http://www.cap.ca/pic/archives/64.2(2008)/Apr08-offprint-Whitmore.pdf)
is available from the \"Physics in Canada\" website.

The article appeared in the [April-June issue](http://www.cap.ca/pic/archives/64.2(2008)/source.html) of the journal, an issue dedicated to HPC and also containing articles by ACENET users David Clarke, Ivan Saika-Voivod and Peter H. Poole.

[Dr. Whitmore](http://www.physics.umanitoba.ca/~whitmore/whitmore.html) was previously a Professor in Memorial\'s Department of Physics
and Physical Oceanography. He is now the Dean of Science at the University of Manitoba.
