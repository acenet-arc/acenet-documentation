# Placentia

Placentia is a general-purpose computing cluster located at Memorial University.
Between 2006 and 2019 it was one of ACENET's primary resources.

In 2019 it was withdrawn from general service.
ACENET continues to manage the surviving equipment
for the benefit of some Memorial University
research groups who contributed hardware.
