# Getting an account

Login accounts on ACENET HPC clusters are of two types:
1. [DRAC](https://www.alliancecan.ca/) accounts, also known as CCDB accounts
2. Paid accounts

If you are a professor at a Canadian institution eligible for tri-council funding,
or if you are sponsored by such a professor, then you can obtain a DRAC
account.  Please visit [https://docs.alliancecan.ca/wiki/Apply_for_a_CCDB_account](https://docs.alliancecan.ca/wiki/Apply_for_a_CCDB_account)
for more details.

If you are interested in paid access to ACENET's Siku HPC cluster, see
[https://www.ace-net.ca/compute-resources/](https://www.ace-net.ca/compute-resources/) "Commercial Access" for more
information, then [contact us](https://www.ace-net.ca/contact/) to obtain
an account.
